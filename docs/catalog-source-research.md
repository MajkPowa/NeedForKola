# Vehicle catalogue source research — 2026-09-05

Scope: the requested 53 make groups / 401 model families, generations, facelifts and body styles through 2026. Engines and equipment grades are excluded. This is a source assessment, not a completeness certificate.

## Selected open seed

[gor3a/vehicle-makes-models](https://github.com/gor3a/vehicle-makes-models) publishes generation facts as ODbL 1.0 and code as MIT. The [data licence](https://github.com/gor3a/vehicle-makes-models/blob/main/LICENSE-DATA) requires attribution to the project and autoevolution.com, disclosure of adaptations, and access to adapted data under ODbL. The repository reports 164 makes, 2,621 models and 7,169 generation records.

Pinned revision inspected: `b4965631140fa82404359167621274601da69a5b`, dated 2026-07-02. Do not confuse its weekly-update promise with observed data freshness.

Download: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gor3a/vehicle-makes-models/b4965631140fa82404359167621274601da69a5b/data/json/<make>.json`. The complete `all.json` is about 84 MB; per-make files are preferable.

Hierarchy: group → makes → models → generations → engines. Generation fields are `name`, `yearStart`, `yearEnd`, `bodyType`; strip engines for the website. In inspected BMW records, `bodyType` is null and body names are usually embedded in `name`. There is no explicit chassis code or facelift flag. Family mapping must combine entries such as `2 Series`, `2 Series Active Tourer`, `2 Series Gran` and `2 Series Gran Tourer`.

Observed defects include duplicate Z4 records, missing recent Z4 periods, and obsolete open-ended dates. Retain source provenance and qualify coverage. Do not convert each successive period automatically into a facelift or a new generation.

## Other candidates assessed

- [lifeofcapo/car-api](https://github.com/lifeofcapo/car-api), MIT: `car-brands.json` includes chassis codes and occasional facelift labels. Inspected revision `0a7092f9a37b179115bcc034d526155688ba9405` dated 2026-08-11 contains duplicates and clear taxonomy errors, including a 1975 E21 under M3 and F32 under Series 3 Coupé. Unsuitable for a blind enrichment join.
- [vehiclesdb/vehiclesdb](https://github.com/vehiclesdb/vehiclesdb), CC BY 4.0: primary-register-based make/model identity, body types and regional presence. Its [schema](https://github.com/vehiclesdb/vehiclesdb/blob/main/SCHEMA.md) expressly describes generations and variants as reserved, unpopulated layers. Useful identity cross-check, not the requested detailed source.
- [plowman/open-vehicle-db](https://github.com/plowman/open-vehicle-db): US make/model/year/style data through 2027. Its hierarchy does not supply global generation and facelift identities.
- [Dazmac-PTY-LTD/car-data-specifications](https://github.com/Dazmac-PTY-LTD/car-data-specifications): broad generation/body fields, but no open data licence; README states auto-data.net owns the specifications. Not selected for bulk redistribution.
- [Cars Base API documentation](https://cars-base.ru/api2): models, generations and body configurations have a suitable hierarchy. Complete detailed access requires a purchased token. The unauthenticated `/full` route supplies makes/models only; `token=test` is a demo.

## Integration requirements

1. Preserve all requested model families even if their detailed provider match is missing.
2. Map exact provider model aliases explicitly; do not fabricate entries to satisfy a count.
3. Include source revision and raw source name for every imported record.
4. Body variants and chronological periods must remain distinguishable. A period is not proof of a facelift.
5. Fill gaps and correct high-impact discrepancies using manufacturer sources, with per-record links and a stated verification date.
6. Limit the visible year picker to sourced model periods, capped at 2026. Distinguish production, presentation, order opening and deliveries in `startBasis`; these can occur in different years. A null source end date is not independent evidence of current production.
7. Publish the compact adapted dataset and its attribution alongside the app. Model images and 3D assets require separate provenance; a textual catalogue does not provide those assets.

## Delivered mapping and reviewed supplements

`data/model-crosswalk.json` contains all 401 requested family keys. The selected seed supplies periods for 390 families, with 2,876 source records before duplicate removal. The 11 seed gaps and mapping decisions are preserved in `data/mapping-report.json`; manufacturer supplements can fill some gaps without changing that report of the original seed.

`data/curated-vehicles.json`, generated by `tools/build-curated-vehicles.py`, contains 62 reviewed entries across BMW Z4 and řada 3, Škoda Fabia/Octavia/Superb, and Volkswagen Golf/Passat. One entry can contain multiple bodies only when its model-phase years agree. Manufacturer links are attached per record, with supporting links for body-specific dates. This is a bounded supplement, not evidence that all generations and facelifts across the complete catalogue have been verified.

Key primary chronologies include the Škoda [Octavia timeline](https://cdn.skoda-storyboard.com/2024/05/Octavia_History_a6eedf18.pdf), [Fabia timeline](https://cdn.skoda-storyboard.com/2021/04/SKODA_FABIA_History.pdf), [Superb timeline](https://cdn.skoda-storyboard.com/2023/10/Skoda_Superb_History_14928d05.pdf) and Volkswagen's [Golf archive](https://www.volkswagen-newsroom.com/en/images/topics/model-archive-golf-34). These timelines establish phase names and dates; supplementary production and launch announcements establish different body start/end dates.

Examples corrected against manufacturer sources: Z4 G29 production starts in 2018, with deliveries in 2019 and a facelift launched in November 2022; Fabia III Combi continues through 2022; Fabia I Sedan ends in March 2008; Octavia IV liftback opens Czech orders in 2020 after Combi; Superb IV Combi production begins in December 2023, with liftback following in 2024; the last Passat B8 Variant from Emden is produced in March 2024. Records retain the exact basis rather than pretending all dates mean a model year.

`replacesSourceNames` enumerates exact seed labels replaced by reviewed periods, scoped by family and compatible body. This avoids deleting distinct Scout/Alltrack derivatives merely because they launched within a year of the base model. Historical Golf periods with unresolved internal changes and the broad Fabia I Sedan period have `facelift: null`; this means unclassified, not pre-facelift. Golf III/IV Cabriolet and Golf VII Variant have separately sourced facelift splits.

Final integration review found no leftover imported base periods beneath these replacements and no duplicate verified Superb IV liftback. Octavia Scout records retain wagon bodies. Fabia Combi stops in 2022 and Z4 G29 in 2026. The retained imported Golf five-door releases of 2007 and 2010 have ambiguous regional context; they are deliberately not merged into the reviewed European five-door chronology. Earlier BMW periods and unreviewed derivative periods still carry imported rather than manufacturer-verified provenance.
